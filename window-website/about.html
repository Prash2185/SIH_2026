Our Story

Built on Honesty.
Driven by Craft.

Crystal Clean began in 2010 when founder Vikram Mehta noticed a simple truth — most window cleaning services in India were doing a mediocre job at premium prices.

Armed with years in facilities management and a determination to do better, he started with a single van and one promise to his first customer: "If it's not perfect, we come back."

That promise has never been broken. Today, Crystal Clean serves 3,200+ clients across Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, and Delhi.
