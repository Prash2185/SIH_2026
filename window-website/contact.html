Frequently Asked.

Do you clean interior windows as well?

Yes, absolutely. We offer comprehensive interior and exterior cleaning packages for both residential and commercial properties, taking care to protect your floors and furniture.

What products do you use?

We use purified water systems and eco-certified, biodegradable cleaning solutions that are completely safe for your pets, plants, and family.

Are your technicians insured?

Yes. Every technician is fully trained, background-checked, and covered by our comprehensive £20M public liability insurance.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

For exterior-only cleans, you do not need to be present as long as we have access to the property. For interior cleans, we require someone to be home to allow us access.
